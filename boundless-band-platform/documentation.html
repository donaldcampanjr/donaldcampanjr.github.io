03 / Band Profile

One source for artist identity

Enter the artist name, project type, tagline, origin, website, contact and booking email, logo, hero image, short biography, and full biography. HTTPS URLs and field-linked validation protect the saved record. Project type describes the act; it does not grant account authority.

04 / Appearance

Style packs with accessible output

Choose a style pack, light and dark semantic colors, typography, buttons, shadows, motion, spacing, and heading treatment. Effective quick colors are adjusted separately for light and dark surfaces when needed, and invalid custom palettes are rejected at WCAG AA contrast. Visitors retain System, Light, and Dark preferences.

05 / Members

People and credits, not permissions

Create public profiles for real members and collaborators, including role, biography, image, order, and publication state. Solo artists may leave this collection empty. An Artist Member account is a separate, explicitly linked Tour Manager identity with assignment-scoped access.

06 / Music

Public release catalog

Manage release type, date, artwork, description, label, catalog number, display order, and Spotify, Apple Music, Bandcamp, YouTube Music, or primary links. Public release pages and archives remain editable through native Divi 5.

07 / Music Manager / Gated

Private masters to public derivatives

Music Manager stores original audio outside the web root, scans it with ClamAV, creates MP3 derivatives with FFmpeg, manages rights and takedown state, builds playlists, and exports offline RouteNote and DistroKid CSV metadata. It never collects distributor credentials or submits a release.

Disabled until accepted.

Private storage, scanner signatures, FFmpeg, worker execution, quotas, authorization, playback, takedown cleanup, and private-audio backup restore must pass on staging first.

08 / Shows

Publish only fan-facing facts

Shows own date, time, timezone, venue, public address, ticket link and label, price note, description, and cancellation state. Tour Manager creates only a draft, only after an authorized operator explicitly converts a confirmed booking.

09 / Merch

Build measured products

WooCommerce owns products, inventory, tax, cart, checkout, payments, refunds, and accounts. Artist HQ simplifies publication and supplies draft shipping demos for apparel, CDs, vinyl, posters, accessories, and bundles. Replace every demo value with the real product, SKU, inventory, weight, and sealed parcel dimensions before publishing.

10 / Shipping

Measure first. Charge once.

Every physical product needs its packed weight and outside dimensions, or an explicitly reviewed carrier default. The fulfillment origin must be one complete, internally consistent sender address.

USPS

Domestic and international rates, labels, customs documents, tracking, voids and refunds, returns, SCAN forms, and pickups require approved account capabilities.

UPS

Rates, U.S. and Puerto Rico address validation, domestic labels, declared value, tracking, voids, and domestic return drafts use the configured UPS account.

Unknown carrier outcome: inspect the carrier account before retrying. Never submit a potentially billable action twice because a browser timed out.

11 / Orders

Fulfill with durable intent

Create a shipment draft from final packed measurements, request current rates, review insurance, and confirm the selected label charge. Tracking, customer notices, refunds, returns, USPS SCAN Forms, and USPS pickups remain attached to the WooCommerce order without overwriting earlier shipments.

Pending or unknown is not failure. Reconcile the carrier account and use the available audited action. Do not blindly replay a label, refund, manifest, or pickup request.

12 / Booking

Publish contact routes, not deal terms

Set the public booking email, contact email, and website route. Keep offers, personal contacts, contracts, travel, rooms, and settlements inside private Tour Manager records.

13 / SEO + Social

Control discovery and public profiles

SEO manages titles, descriptions, HTTPS canonical URLs, social cards, and robots settings for the site and owned archives. Social accepts supported HTTPS profiles and renders only enabled entries in the public footer.

14 / Fan Access / Gated

Identity, policy evidence, and consent

The Fan capability is frontend-only and can coexist with WooCommerce Customer or WordPress Subscriber identity. Access requires verified email and acceptance of current operator-authored Terms and Privacy versions. Newsletter consent is separate, optional, unchecked, and recorded with its exact notice version.

The WooCommerce My Account Fan dashboard remains fail-closed until the code release constant, self-hosted mail transport, consent storage, policy versions, canonical policy URLs, and Administrator enablement all pass. The platform does not provide legal text.

15 / Email Marketing / Gated

First-party campaigns, local transport

Artist Managers create consent-aware HTML and plain-text campaigns. A bounded background queue rechecks Fan authority, verified email, consent, and suppression before every handoff. Delivery uses WordPress wp_mail() and the operator's self-hosted MTA; there is no delivery-vendor API.

Only Administrators configure sender identity or enable delivery. SPF, DKIM, DMARC, reverse DNS, TLS, queue monitoring, bounce and complaint operations, unsubscribe, privacy, backup, restore, and controlled staging delivery must pass first. An accepted message was handed to the local MTA; it is not proof of destination delivery.

17 / Master Tour Manager

Advance the show privately

  1. 1
    Create the private tour

    Set the date range, planning status, home base, and working timezone. No public Show is created.

  2. 2
    Advance the booking

    Track inquiry, holds, contacts, offer, deposit, balance, settlement, and internal notes.

  3. 3
    Build the day

    Apply club, festival, travel, rehearsal, press, or off-day templates, then replace estimates with confirmed times.

  4. 4
    Assign the work

    Give tasks an owner, due date, priority, status, tour, and booking context.

  5. 5
    Share the minimum

    Generate revocable calendar or day-sheet links. Keep private money, contacts, notes, and documents in Artist HQ.

  6. 6
    Create a Show draft intentionally

    Only a confirmed, authorized booking action can create a Core Show draft. Publication is never automatic.

Deactivation preserves operations.

Tour records and protected files are removed only through explicit plugin deletion. Back up the database and protected document directory before uninstalling.

18 / Public Site

Home, Music, Shows, Merch, Videos, About, Booking, Journal, Sitemap, Cart, Checkout, My Account, Site Settings, and policy links form the owned public experience. 19 / Distribution and owner operations

Use each channel for one job

GitHub Pages is the public product and documentation surface. It can link to checkout but must not process payments, license secrets, or customer data. The HTTPS store domain owns checkout and accounts. WooCommerce Orders and Analytics own sales and refunds, WooCommerce Subscriptions owns recurring billing, and Boundless Artist Store owns licenses, site activations, protected releases, and update health.

21 / Licensed updates

  • Download URLs are short-lived and bound to an active license and site.

  • Published versions are immutable; corrections ship as a newer version.
