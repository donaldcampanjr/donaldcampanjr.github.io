01 / The pressure

The show is confirmed.
The work is everywhere.

Fan sites, store orders, shipping labels, booking threads, call times, contracts, and settlements rarely live in the same place. That fragmentation costs attention exactly when the artist needs it most.

Boundless Artist puts the public experience and private operation on one accountable WordPress foundation without mixing their data.

Public site Merch store Tour desk Fulfillment One artist system

02 / Boundless Artist Core

The artist owns the stack.

Structured content and operational controls inside WordPress. WooCommerce remains the commerce authority. Divi 5 remains the visual editor.

A

One public identity

Members, music, shows, videos, press, booking, journal, and store pages share one editable design system.

  • Native Divi 5 layouts
  • Structured artist data
  • Dark, light, and system modes

B

Commerce without the maze

Focused Artist HQ workflows simplify products, orders, fulfillment, tracking, and customer communication.

  • WooCommerce authority
  • Merch starter patterns
  • Carrier-aware fulfillment

C

Roles with real boundaries

Owners keep administration. Artist Managers run the operation. Customers remain in the fan experience.

  • Purpose-built permissions
  • Private operational records
  • No shared admin free-for-all

D / Runtime gated

Protected music workflow

Private masters, malware scanning, MP3 derivatives, track metadata, playlists, release lifecycle, and offline distributor exports.

  • Private originals
  • FFmpeg derivatives
  • RouteNote and DistroKid CSV

E / Runtime gated

Fan identity and portal

A frontend-only Fan capability, verified email, versioned policy acceptance, optional consent, suppression, export, and erasure.

  • WooCommerce account portal
  • No Artist HQ access
  • Consent stays separate

F / Runtime gated

First-party email

Consent-aware HTML and plain-text campaigns use WordPress wp_mail(), a bounded queue, and the operator's self-hosted MTA.

  • No delivery-vendor API
  • One-click unsubscribe
  • Fail-closed readiness

Gated means implemented, not automatically enabled. Fan Access, Email Marketing, and Music Manager stay off until their runtime, privacy, recovery, and staging acceptance requirements pass.
